# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with React islands for interactive calculators and forms, Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel via GitHub auto-deploy. Delegated: chosen so every page has its own indexable URL (the previous React single-page app served all pages from "/"). Forms post to Formspree; scheduling uses Calendly.

## Users

- **Luxury residential clients:** homeowners selling and buyers purchasing in Miami-Dade and Broward (Coral Gables, Coconut Grove, Brickell, Edgewater, Pinecrest and similar), including Latin American and international buyers who value a bilingual (English/Spanish) advisor.
- **Commercial and land clients:** owners of development land, assemblages, industrial and transit-adjacent sites, and the developers and investors who buy them; they need valuation, strategy and feasibility before they need a listing.
- **Real estate agents (secondary, recruiting):** agents and team leaders evaluating Epique Realty. Served on /join, not on the client-facing pages.

## Product Purpose

The marketing and lead-generation site for Jaelly & Dennis Prieto, licensed Florida real estate sales associates with Epique Realty. Success: a luxury seller or commercial owner leaves convinced the Prietos understand their property better than the agent they were going to call, and books a consultation or requests a valuation.

## Positioning

Property diagnosis before marketing: Dennis leads with data, MLS and public-record analysis and a construction background to find what is actually holding a property's value back (municipality errors, permit and roof records, zoning and Live Local Act potential) before proposing a strategy. Bilingual team with Epique Estates luxury marketing behind it.

## Operating Context

Clients arrive from referrals, LinkedIn, direct outreach (letters, calls, texts), and search. Commercial owners are often approached first with a Broker Opinion of Value (BOV) or offering memorandum. Consultations are booked through Calendly; inquiries arrive via Formspree to both partners' inboxes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Brokerage: Epique Realty, Inc. (supervising broker Hank Bertodatto). Every page must identify Epique Realty as the brokerage.
- Dennis Prieto: Florida sales associate SL3347930. Jaelly Prieto: Florida sales associate SL3362729.
- Commercial & Land is presented as advisory and analysis (land and assemblage strategy, valuations/BOVs, development feasibility, Live Local Act analysis). No implied commercial transaction volume.
- Epique may not market a property it is not listing (broker rule). No listing-style pages for properties the Prietos do not represent; new-development and off-market items need broker sign-off.
- No closing counts or sales volume on the site until the track record grows.
- Client-facing documents and legally sensitive copy require broker review before use.
- Recruiting content (Epique compensation model, calculators, revenue share, Canada calculator) lives under /join.

## Brand Commitments

- Official Epique gold logo artwork must be used, never a text-rendered Epique name. Assets: brand/epique-wordmark-gold, brand/epique-realty-stacked-gold, brand/epique-q-mark-gold.

## Product Principles

1. Diagnosis before pitch: show how the Prietos think about a property, not adjectives about service.
2. Every claim is checkable. No numbers, quotes or results that a client could not verify.
3. Two clients, one standard: luxury residential and commercial/land get equal weight; agent recruiting never appears in front of a client.
4. Discretion is part of the service: off-market and client details stay private.
